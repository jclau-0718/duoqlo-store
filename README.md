# DUOQLO Store


## 📁 Project Structure
- products/ -> Stores product images
- controller/ -> Stores controller classes that handles UI logic
- model/ -> Stores data classes and database logic
- view/ -> Stores JavaFX UI pages

### controller/
Handles logic operations and connects the UI with the backend logic.

### Sign Up Account
1. Click "Sign Up"
2. Enter your personal info details, then click "Next"
3. Enter your log in details, then click "Sign Up"

### Log In
1. Enter the registered username
2. Enter your account password
3. Click "Log In"

### Purchase Products (Add to Cart)
1. Click on the product card you wish to purchase
2. Click "Add to Cart"
3. Click on the orange 🛒 icon at the top right
4. Click "Checkout" and select a payment method
5. Click "Confirm"

### Purchase Products (Buy Now)
1. Click on the product card you wish to purchase
2. Click "Buy Now"
3. Click "Checkout" and select a payment method
4. Click "Confirm"

### View Orders
1. Click on the orange 🧾 icon at the top right
2. Click "Cancel Order" to cancel the order
3. Click "Download Receipt" to download a receipt of the order

### View Profile
1. Click on the orange 👤 icon at the top right
2. Click "View Profile"
3. Choose either "Edit Info", "Edit Address" or "Edit Credentials"
4. Update your information and click "Update"

### Back To Dashboard
1. Click on the orange logo "DUOQLO" at the top left

### Deactivate Account
1. Click on the orange 👤 icon at the top right
2. Click "Deactivate Account"
3. Click "Confirm"

### Log Out
1. Click on the orange 👤 icon at the top right
2. Click "Log Out"

### Default Admin Account
To access admin features, use the following default account:
```text
- Username: admin123
- Password: Admin@123
```

### Log In
1. Enter the registered username
2. Enter your account password
3. Click "Log In"

### View Customer/Admin Data
1. Click on the "Total Customers"/"Total Admins" card at the top
2. Click "Update" to update the customer/admin info
3. Click "Deactivate" to deactivate the customer/admin
4. Click "Reactivate" to reactivate the deactivated customer/admin

### View Product Data
1. Click on the "Total Products" card at the top
2. Click "+ Add Product" to add a new product
3. Click "View" to view product details
4. Within the product details section, click "Next" to view the sizes details section
5. Click "Update" in the sizes details section to update the product details
6. Click "Remove" to remove the product
7. Click "◀️ Back to Table" to return to the product table

### View Gender/Category Data
1. Click on the "Total Products" card at the top
2. Click "+ Add Gender" or "+ Add Category" to add a new gender/category
3. Click "Update" to update the gender/category details
4. Click "Remove" to remove the gender/category
5. Click "◀️ Back to Table" to return to the gender/category table

### View Order Data
1. Click on the "Total Orders" card at the top
2. Click "View" to view the order details
3. Click "Done" to set the order as done

### View Sales
1. Click on the "Total Revenue (RM)" card at the top
2. Click the filter & frequency buttons above the sales table to view the relevant sales
3. Click "📄 Export to PDF" to export the sales table and charts as a pdf file

### Reinitialize Page
1. Click on the orange logo "DUOQLO" at the top left

### Log Out
1. Click "Log Out" at the top right
